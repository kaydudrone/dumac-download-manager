Download button for Safari

Drag this button up into Safari's Favorites bar (under the address bar):

⬇ Download this video
  1. Can't see the Favorites bar? Press ⇧ ⌘ B.
  2. Drag the blue button onto the bar and let go.
  3. On any video page (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook…), click it.
  4. The first time, Safari asks "Do you want to allow this page to open Dumac Download Manager?". Click Allow.
  5. Dumac Download Manager opens with the link filled in. Pick a quality and click Start Download.

Tip: on a YouTube video inside a playlist, the button downloads just that one video.
